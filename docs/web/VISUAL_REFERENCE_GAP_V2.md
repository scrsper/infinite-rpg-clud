## What the supplied frames actually show

- **Photos 1–4, open-field action:** a low third-person camera puts the character in the lower middle and leaves hill silhouettes against the sky. The terrain rises behind the subject, so grass, scattered rock groups, middle-distance trees and a distant ridge overlap. Fine grass forms broad continuous masses around readable action space. Small flowers vary those masses; they do not dominate. Rounded, asymmetrical tree crowns and clustered rock shelves break the smooth hills. Bright yellow-green sunlit grass contrasts with darker contact shadows and a cyan sky. Materials are mostly matte; the glossy creatures are focal exceptions. The characters have clear limbs, layered clothes/hair and strong poses. Shadows are fairly defined in these frames, not uniformly soft.
- **Photos 5–7, forest trail:** a closer trailing camera sits above the shoulders with a modest downward pitch. The winding path is the principal negative space. Near rocks, ferns and trunks crop into the frame; staggered trunks, boulders and vegetation create successive depth layers. Warm directional light makes bright patches on the path against cool shaded vegetation. Haze compresses contrast in the distance. Rough stone has large readable planes and softened edges; moss, leaf litter and grasses bind rocks to soil. Detail gathers at boundaries and leaves the route open. The pack/shoulders form a readable character silhouette even in shade.
- **Photos 8–10, isometric clearing:** steep oblique framing keeps the action central while tree crowns, rock terraces, stairs and fences form an irregular frame. Golden worn paths divide darker grass into purposeful shapes. Tall grass grows in a few large patches with ragged edges. Orange foliage, muted green ground and purple-gray rock/shadow establish a narrow palette. Broad beveled rock forms are more legible than fine surface noise. Warm pools of light, soft atmospheric overlays and restrained edge glow separate the scene into layers. Combat effects are locally bright; the underlying environment remains readable.

These stills do not establish exact camera height, FOV, exposure, roughness values or shadow settings. Bloom versus baked/glowing artwork cannot be conclusively separated. Motion cadence, wind, animation blending and particle behavior require video; only pose and effects silhouettes can be assessed here. No useful water or medieval-building reference appears in the supplied images, so those details must come from Torn Veil's own setting and canonical structures.
